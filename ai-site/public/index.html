FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need to know how to code or design?

No. You describe your business in plain language and edit the result visually. If you do like code, you can export clean HTML and CSS.

Can I edit the site after it is generated?

Yes. Every headline, image, color and section is editable, and you can reorder or add sections at any time.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes, on Pro and Business. Starter sites are published on a free pagewright.app subdomain.

What happens if I cancel?

You keep access until the end of your billing period, and you can export your site whenever you like.

Is the generator on this page the real thing?

It is a demo that sketches an outline from keywords. The full product writes complete copy and designs.
